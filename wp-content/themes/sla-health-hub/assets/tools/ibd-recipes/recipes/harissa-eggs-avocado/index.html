🌅 Harissa Fried Eggs & Avocado on Sourdough

A bold, flavourful breakfast with spiced harissa eggs on creamy avocado toast.

⚠️Allergen notice
  • Eggs
  • Gluten (sourdough — unless GF)
  • Sesame (if added)

Always check ingredient packaging — formulations vary. Omit or substitute any ingredient you react to.

Ingredients

Serves
1
  • ·1 slice sourdough bread (use gluten-free if required)
  • ·½ avocado
  • ·Squeeze of lemon juice
  • ·2 eggs
  • ·1 tsp olive oil
  • ·1 tsp harissa paste
  • ·Sea salt and black pepper
  • ·Optional: chilli flakes, sesame seeds, fresh herbs

Method

  1. 1

    Toast the sourdough bread until golden.

  2. 2

    Heat olive oil in a small pan over medium heat.

  3. 3

    Crack in the eggs and cook for 2 minutes.

  4. 4

    Spoon harissa paste around the eggs and swirl gently. Cook to your preferred doneness.

  5. 5

    Mash avocado with a pinch of salt, pepper and lemon juice.

  6. 6

    Spread avocado on toast and top with the harissa eggs.

  7. 7

    Finish with optional garnishes.

🌿 Why This Recipe Supports Gut Health

Avocado provides prebiotic fibre and healthy fats, while eggs deliver complete protein and important B vitamins for gut lining support.

Sourdough fermentation breaks down some FODMAPs (notably fructans), which is why genuine long-fermented sourdough is often better tolerated than standard wheat bread — but only if you do not require strict gluten avoidance. Harissa is generally well-tolerated in remission; reduce or omit during active flare.
