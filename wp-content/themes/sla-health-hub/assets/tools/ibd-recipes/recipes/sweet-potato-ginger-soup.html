Sweet Potato & Ginger Soup

🍽️ Sweet Potato & Ginger Soup

A velvety, warming soup with sweet potato, carrots and ginger — comforting and easy to digest.

⚠️Allergen notice
  • Tree nuts (almond milk, if used)
  • Coconut (if used)

Always check ingredient packaging — formulations vary. Omit or substitute any ingredient you react to.

Ingredients

Serves
3
  • ·3 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and diced
  • ·2 carrots, chopped
  • ·1 small onion, chopped
  • ·1 tbsp fresh grated ginger
  • ·750 ml vegetable broth
  • ·1 tbsp olive oil
  • ·½ tsp turmeric
  • ·Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • ·50–100 ml unsweetened coconut milk or almond milk (optional, for creaminess)

Method

  1. 1

    Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.

  2. 2

    Add onion and sauté for 3–4 minutes until softened.

  3. 3

    Stir in ginger and turmeric; cook 1 minute until fragrant.

  4. 4

    Add sweet potatoes and carrots.

  5. 5

    Pour in vegetable broth; bring to a gentle boil.

  6. 6

    Reduce heat and simmer for 25–30 minutes until vegetables are soft.

  7. 7

    Blend until smooth.

  8. 8

    Stir in plant-based milk for a creamier texture if desired.

  9. 9

    Season with salt and pepper.

💡 Tip: If you are sensitive to onion, use the green part of a leek as a lower FODMAP alternative.
🍽️ Serving: Serve with toasted sourdough or gluten-free bread.

🌿 Why This Recipe Supports Gut Health

Sweet potatoes provide fibre and beta-carotene, while ginger has traditionally been used to support digestion and reduce digestive discomfort.

Pureeing reduces the mechanical fibre load — a useful strategy during mild flares when whole-form vegetables are poorly tolerated. Sweet potato is a source of vitamin A precursors important for epithelial repair. Onion contains fructans (high FODMAP); the leek-greens swap in the tip preserves flavour without the fructan.
