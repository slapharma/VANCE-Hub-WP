Gluten-Free Chicken Tacos with Avocado Slaw

🍽️ Gluten-Free Chicken Tacos with Avocado Slaw

Fresh, flavourful tacos with seasoned chicken and a creamy avocado slaw in gluten-free corn tortillas.

Ingredients

Serves
2

For the chicken

  • ·2 chicken breasts, sliced into strips
  • ·1 tbsp olive oil
  • ·1 tsp ground cumin
  • ·1 tsp paprika
  • ·½ tsp garlic powder
  • ·Juice of ½ lime
  • ·Salt and black pepper, to taste

For the avocado slaw

  • ·1 cup shredded cabbage
  • ·½ avocado
  • ·Juice of ½ lime
  • ·1 tbsp olive oil
  • ·Small handful fresh coriander, chopped
  • ·Pinch salt

To serve

  • ·4 small gluten-free corn tortillas

Method

  1. 1

    Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.

  2. 2

    Add chicken, cumin, paprika and garlic powder. Cook 8–10 minutes until cooked through.

  3. 3

    Squeeze over lime juice and season with salt and pepper.

  4. 4

    Mash avocado with lime juice and olive oil until creamy.

  5. 5

    Add shredded cabbage and coriander; toss to combine.

  6. 6

    Warm tortillas in a dry pan for 30–60 seconds per side.

  7. 7

    Fill each tortilla with chicken and avocado slaw. Serve immediately.

🌿 Why This Recipe Supports Gut Health

Lean chicken provides high-quality protein, while cabbage and avocado contribute fibre and healthy fats that support overall digestive health.

Cabbage is a brassica — these vegetables contain glucosinolates with potential anti-inflammatory effects, but raw brassicas can be gas-producing. Lightly massaging shredded cabbage with lime juice and a little salt softens the fibres and improves tolerability. Choose tortillas labelled "100% corn" to confirm absence of wheat blends.
